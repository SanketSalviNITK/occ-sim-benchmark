## C2 (IMX174) — strictly scoped wording (audit r7 §3; benchmark-r8)
> "In one assumed configuration — IMX174 model, P33, brightness 1/128, exposure 1/1500, ASSUMED lens f = 6 mm, f/2.0 focused at 1.0 m, 65 mm 8×8 panel on axis, shared corner finder, canonical seeds — HNCSM v2 has the highest simulated goodput at 3.0 m and 3.25 m (binding margin +150.0 [126.6, 173.4] and +107.6 [86.3, 129.0] bps). This is not a robust result: in a 36-cell factorial over focus, focal length, f-number and brightness it holds only in this default cell (1/36 at each distance), and it disappears under an 80 mm panel, under 30° yaw at 3.25 m, under CFA crosstalk ×2 and under per-scheme tuned exposure. The comparison is relative only. With an added signal-proportional noise term fitted to external anchors (σ_mult 0.024–0.15, per-pixel or per-frame, outside the Holm family) v2 stays first and the margin stays positive (minimum +100.8 [76.8, 124.8] at 3.0 m and +96.0 [72.6, 119.4] at 3.25 m), but it shrinks by 36–49 bps at 3.0 m at σ_mult = 0.15 because noise reduces the shared finder's gross errors on the baselines; part of the noise-free margin is therefore an artefact of noise-free rendering. With oracle corners v2 is not first at 3.0 m under any tested noise level, so at 3.0 m the advantage lies in finder reliability, not demodulation; at 3.25 m this attribution holds only without added noise (oracle margin n.s. for σ_mult ≥ 0.05). No claim is made for real hardware or for other optics."

Supporting statistics (outside the quote): across all four seed sets the minimum margin is +148.2 (3.0 m) and +107.6 (3.25 m), Holm-significant in the 56-claim family; 3.5 m is not claimed (canonical +4.3 [−10.1, 19.1]); oracle corners on the same seeds: −114.1 [−141.9, −86.6] vs OOK-8x8 at 3.0 m and −24.0 [−45.0, −3.6] vs QCD-style at 3.25 m (unadjusted; n.s. under σ_mult ≥ 0.05, `r8/mult_binding.csv`). Under every tested σ_mult (0.024/0.05/0.15, per-pixel and per-frame) v2 remains first at 3.0 and 3.25 m and the binding margin stays significantly positive; it shrinks significantly only at σ_mult = 0.15 at 3.0 m (Δ −49.2 [−73.8, −25.2] per-pixel, −36.0 [−60.0, −12.0] per-frame). The r8 noise runs are outside the Holm family.

lambert_m is not a factor that "keeps" C2: on axis it has no effect by construction (cos^m(0) = 1); at 30° yaw (`r7/lambert_yaw30.csv`) no verdict changes.

## QCD-OCC BER plateau (R8)
**QCD-OCC BER plateau (R8; `r7/qcd_plateau_diag.csv`, 1000 symbols per cell, QCD-OCC unit at 2.0 m, f_tx 25).** The plateau is a **deterministic error floor, not noise-limited**: the base BER stays at ≈1.5–1.8e-2 from brightness 1/160 to 1/1024 (1.52e-2, 1.83e-2, 1.56e-2, 1.61e-2, 1.56e-2, 1.48e-2) while the model's shot SNR (G) falls from 62.9 to 25.1. About 65–75 % of the bit errors are on the **R bit** (share_R_bit 0.65–0.74 over that range; 0.76 at 1/128). It is **not rolling-shutter mixing** (t_row = 0: 1.40–1.56e-2; f_tx = 15: 1.27–1.54e-2 over 1/160–1/1024) and **not CFA crosstalk** (cfa = I: 1.96e-2 at 1/256, 1.79e-2 at 1/1024, with the R-bit share rising to 0.82–0.86). The **low BER at 1/96–1/128 depends on saturation clipping**: base 1.6e-4 at 1/96 and 7.1e-3 at 1/128, where peak B is 1.66× / 1.24× full well; with the clipping removed (no_clip12) the floor appears there too (1.44e-2 at 1/96, 1.61e-2 at 1/128, 1.66e-2 at 1/64). Consequence: the published 1.13e-2 is "bracketed" by the grid only because of a saturation transition, not because the model reproduces the link. R9 traced the floor to the receiver's Bayer under-sampling (4×4 canonical window), not to the R-channel decision — see the R9 section.

## QCD-OCC error floor traced (R9, reviewer must-fix 2; benchmark-r9)
**Root cause (R9; `r9/qcd_floor_noisefree.csv`, `r9/qcd_win6_crn.csv`, `r9/qcd_win6_c2.csv`).** The ≈1.5e-2 floor is a **receiver sampling artefact, not physics and not a decoder threshold, colour-mapping or white-balance bug**. At 2 m the LED pitch is ≈4.2 sensor px (lit disc ≈2.5 px); the shared sampler (`rx/sampler.py`, WIN = 4 of G = 8 canonical px per LED) averages only ≈2×2 sensor px per LED, i.e. ≈1 R and ≈1 B Bayer site. Malvar–He–Cutler interpolation of the sparse R/B planes then gives a sub-pixel-phase-dependent colour error that scales with the signal, so the BER is brightness-independent; R is worst (lowest QE×CFA response, smallest R decision distance). Evidence: with all noise removed (no shot/read noise, no FPN, no clipping, 16-bit ADC, t_row 0, true corners) the old receiver still gives 1.97–1.98e-2 BER (74 % R-bit) at 1/128, 1/512 and 1/2048, and exactly 0 without the mosaic/demosaic step; genie corners do not remove it (probe). Window 2/4/6/8 → noise-free BER 9.0e-2 / 2.0e-2 / 8e-4 / 1–2e-4.
**Opt-in corrected receiver.** `tx.schemes.QCDOCCWin6` (name `QCD-OCC (paper frame, win6 rx)`): same frame, encoder and nearest-colour decision, 6×6 sampling window (`sample_win`, passed by `phys_eval.decode_symbol`; schemes without `sample_win` use WIN = 4, byte-identical). The default `QCDOCC` is unchanged; every old-receiver array in the CRN rerun is identical to the shipped one (15/15 valid6 cells vs `r6_valid6.npz`, 11/11 plateau cells vs the r7 cache, 3/3 C2 cells vs `review_e2_P33.npz`). The window 6 was chosen as the smallest window that removes the noise-free floor (diagnostic probe, not tuned on the evaluation seeds).
**CRN-paired effect (same frames, B = 10000, seed 0).** valid6 grid (2 m, 2000 symbols): at 1/160–1/256 BER drops from 1.46–1.66e-2 to 0.77–1.50e-3 (all 9 cells significant, Δ ≈ −1.4e-2); at 1/128 from 5.4–6.2e-3 to 2.0–4.0e-4; at 1/96 n.s. Plateau grid (27 lux, 1000 symbols): 1/160–1/1024 now 0.81–1.88e-3 (was 1.48–1.83e-2); 1/2048 1.44e-2 [1.30, 1.58]e-2 (was 2.93e-2); 1/4096 n.s. (0.418). **Validation consequence:** with the corrected receiver no valid6 cell brackets the published 1.13e-2 (0/15 CIs contain it); along the plateau grid it lies between 1/1024 (1.7e-3) and 1/2048 (1.44e-2), i.e. ≈8–16× dimmer than the r6/r7 statement. The r6/r7 "bracketing" of 1.13e-2 at 1/128–1/160 was produced by the sampling floor. **C2 / rankings unchanged:** at the C2 cell (IMX174, P33, 1/128, 3.0/3.25/3.5 m, claims/E2 eval seeds) QCD-OCC has 0 bps goodput with either receiver (BER 0.176→0.172, 0.211→0.221, 0.258→0.261; no error-free 144-bit frame), so the binding margins (+150.0 [126.6, 173.4], +107.6 [86.3, 129.0], +4.3 [−10.1, 19.1] n.s.) and the native ranking (v2 first) are identical whether QCD-OCC-win6 replaces or is added to the comparison set. Not rerun with win6: IMX219 claims, factorial and r8 σ_mult runs (QCD-OCC is not binding there except IMX219, where it already beats v2; a better QCD-OCC receiver can only make those IMX219 margins more negative). The shared WIN = 4 sampler also applies to all benchmark schemes; a window study for the other schemes is not part of r9.

## Signal-proportional noise (R-V1, benchmark-r8)
**Signal-proportional noise (R-V1; `r8/mult_binding.csv`, `r8/mult_goodput.csv`, `r8/mult_finder.csv`, `r8/mult_sigma0_identity.csv`).** Optional `sigma_mult` in `channel.physical.capture` (default 0 → unchanged code path; the σ = 0 control is 234/234 arrays identical to `review_e2_P33.npz`). Model: mean electrons per mosaic site × max(0, 1 + σ z) before PRNU and Poisson; z redrawn for every captured frame (temporally independent); spatial correlation either **per-pixel** (i.i.d.) or **per-frame** (one gain per R/G/B channel over the whole frame); per-LED correlation is not implemented. z comes from a side stream seeded from the capture rng state (the rng is not advanced), so payloads, geometry jitter and camera phase are common across σ levels (CRN-paired with E2); later Poisson draws within a symbol can diverge. Default C2 cell, claims/E2 seeds, eval 1000 + tune 200, B = 10000:

Change vs σ = 0 (joint bootstrap): significant in three cells: σ = 0.15, 3.0 m (−49.2 [−73.8, −25.2] pixel, −36.0 [−60.0, −12.0] frame) and σ = 0.15, 3.5 m per-pixel (+17.6 [1.4, 34.3]; 3.5 m is unclaimed — its positive cells are unadjusted and outside the Holm family); all 3.25 m changes n.s. Oracle-corner binding at 3.0 m stays negative (−66.6 to −121.9); at 3.25 m it becomes n.s. for σ ≥ 0.05 and for σ = 0.024 per-frame. **Baselines gain throughput under noise (S1 explained):** at 3.0 m, σ = 0.15, QCD-style-inspired +61.8 [43.8, 79.8] bps (pixel; 252 → 314) and +49.2 [31.2, 67.8] (frame), v2-base-only +45.0 / +38.4, OOK-8x8 +28.9 (frame), while v2 changes by +12.6 / +13.2 (n.s.). The gain is a **finder effect**: the shared finder's gross-error rate (corner error > 0.5 pitch, per frame) falls for QCD-style from 0.572 to 0.515/0.516 and for v2-base-only from 0.562 to 0.516/0.534, whereas the same schemes' oracle-corner goodput falls (QCD-style 523 → 494/500). Noise dithers the thresholded LED blobs and the refinement centroids, so part of the baselines' finder loss at σ = 0 is a deterministic artefact of noise-free rendering, not a property of the schemes (mult_finder.csv, 3.0 m, σ = 0.15 pixel: QCD-style gross-error rate 0.5725 → 0.515, detector goodput 252 → 314 bps, oracle-corner goodput 523 → 494 bps, finder loss 271 → 181 bps). About 36–49 bps of the 150 bps σ = 0 margin at 3.0 m (roughly a third) therefore depends on noise-free rendering. **Limitations:** per-LED correlated noise (LED-drive nonlinearity correlated over each LED blob) is untested; the per-frame mode may be largely cancelled by the pilot references (inferred, not measured); the σ_mult values are one-point fits to analytic surrogates (plausible, not validated). Detection failures stay 0.

## Factorial key label (R-K)
**Factorial key label (R-K).** `r7_fact.npz` keys are `<cell>|d<d>|1/1500|1/128|<eval|tune>|<scheme>|<field>`; the literal `1/128` is a fixed key slot copied from the E2 key format and is **not** the brightness of the cell. The Tx brightness of a cell is the `brightness=` entry in `<cell>` (e.g. `focus_m=1.0,f_mm=6,f_number=2.0,brightness=1/256|d3.0|1/1500|1/128|eval|…` is a 1/256 cell). Keys were left unchanged so the shipped arrays and checkpoints stay valid; `experiments/r7_report.py` documents the same and `tests/test_r8.py` checks the run meta.
